Who We Are

The Risko Group

PolyX is designed by computational polymer scientists for computational polymer scientists in search of a scalable, intuitive, and adaptable solution for their research. We are committed to supporting the community by providing open, efficient, and scientifically rigorous tools for polymer simulations. Mission Statement

Our mission is to develop intuitive tools that allow computational chemists to efficiently modify, validate, and visualize molecular dynamics input files for polymer simulations, streamlining workflow and accelerating discovery.

GUI

Closely inspect your ITP and coordinate files!

Polymer Extension

Turn a trimer into a polymer of any Length!

SideChain Swapper

Graft your sidechains onto a similar polymer! - Webpage Under Construction

ITP Parser

Package that turns any ITP file into a Python Dictonary. Useful for manipulating,editing, and writing ITP files
